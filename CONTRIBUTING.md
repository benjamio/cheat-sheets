# Contributing

Contributions are welcome, provided they keep the repository concise, readable, and legally clean.

## Scope and style

- Keep each cheat sheet **short enough to print on a single page** (as a rule of thumb: ~1–2 screens of Markdown).
- Prefer **actionable commands and keystrokes** over narrative explanation.
- Use consistent section headings and formatting. Start from the template:
  - `templates/cheatsheet-template.md`

## Content guidelines

- **Write in your own words.** Do not paste large sections from manuals, books, vendor docs, blog posts, or other cheat sheets unless the license explicitly permits it.
- Prefer linking to official docs for deep detail (keep the cheat sheet itself compact).
- Use placeholders in examples:
  - `HOST`, `USER`, `TOKEN`, `/path/to/...`, `NAME`
- Avoid anything that could be sensitive (hostnames, internal URLs, private keys, API tokens, customer names).

## “Last updated”

Include a `Last updated: YYYY-MM-DD` line near the top of each cheat sheet.

## Licensing

By contributing, you agree that your contribution can be published under the repository’s license (CC BY 4.0), unless you explicitly mark a file/section otherwise.
